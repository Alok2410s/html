Rose
  • Roses are a type of flowering shrub that belong to the genus Rosa.
  • They are known for their beautiful and fragrant flowers, which come in a variety of colors including red, pink, white, and yellow.
  • Roses have been cultivated for thousands of years and are often associated with love and romance.
story : rose
        The rose's origin is deeply rooted in Greek and Roman mythology, where the goddess Chloris (or Flora) breathed life into a dying nymph, while Aphrodite (or Venus)
        gave it beauty, Dionysus (or Bacchus) added its scent, and Eros (or Cupid) gifted it to the god of silence to symbolize secrecy and silence. 
        Fossil evidence suggests the rose is an ancient flower that evolved from wild species in the northern hemisphere between 33 million and 23 million years ago, with
        the first scientifically described species, Rosa hilliae, discovered in Colorado in 1883.
About roses
Did you know? The rose is the national flower of the United States, the United Kingdom, and several other countries.
